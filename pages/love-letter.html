♡   A LETTER FOR SONIA   ♡

My dearest Sonia,

Happy Birthday, my love. 💗

If I could give you one thing today, I would give you the chance to see yourself through my eyes. Maybe then you'd understand how special you are, how your presence can make a difficult day feel lighter, and how a simple moment with you can become one of my favourite memories.

You are not only my best friend; you are also the girl who holds such a beautiful place in my heart. I love our friendship, our little conversations, our laughter, and the comfort of knowing that we have something precious between us.

I don't promise that every day will be perfect. I promise to keep learning how to understand you, to listen when you need to talk, to celebrate your wins, and to remind you of your worth when life gets hard. I want our love to feel safe, honest, playful, and kind.

On your birthday, I hope you receive the same warmth you give to the people around you. I hope your dreams get closer, your worries get lighter, and your smile finds a thousand new reasons to appear.

Thank you for being you, Sonia. If I could choose one more time to have you in my life, I'd still choose you—with a happy heart, every single time.

Happy Birthday, my favourite person.
With all my love,
Your Bestie, Your Love ♡
