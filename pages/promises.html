CHAPTER THREE · LITTLE PROMISES

∞

Not perfect promises.
Real ones.

The kind that live in everyday actions, not just beautiful words.

01
耳

I will listen.

I'll make space for your thoughts, your excitement, your worries, and even the things you don't know how to explain yet.

02
☼

I will cheer for you.

Your dreams matter to me. I want to see you grow, shine, and become the person you dream of being.

03
♡

I will choose kindness.

Even when we disagree, I want us to speak with respect, understand each other, and protect the trust we share.

04
✧

I will keep the fun.

More silly jokes, little adventures, unexpected compliments, and reasons to laugh until our cheeks hurt.

♡

My favourite promise?

To keep showing you love in ways you can feel—not only in words you can read.
